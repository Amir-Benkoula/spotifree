## 2. CeFi earn rates and EU availability under MiCA (Coinbase, Kraken, Bitpanda, OKX; USDT and USDe for EU users)

### Takeaway
In the EU, **interest on stablecoins is largely absent from MiCA-authorised platforms**. Coinbase **ended USDC rewards for EEA customers on 1 Dec 2024**, citing MiCA (Article 50 bars interest on e-money tokens). No 2026 reinstatement was found. What remains for EU retail are promotional bonuses (OKX's 8% bonus on net USDC deposits for EEA users ended 31 Jul 2026), products that a third-party review says sit outside MiCA (Bitpanda lending, "up to 7%"), and US-oriented rates (Kraken's 1.75% / 3.75% USDC rewards in April 2026, EU eligibility unverified). **USDT and USDe are not MiCA-authorised**: holding and transferring them is not banned for individuals, but MiCA-licensed venues stopped offering them for trading, and the MiCA transitional deadline of 1 Jul 2026 hardened this. Some sources claim Binance stopped serving EU customers on 1 Jul 2026; that claim is disputed and unverified.

- **Coinbase, EEA:** announced late Nov 2024 that USDC rewards for EEA users would end on **1 Dec 2024**; accrual ran to 30 Nov and payouts were due by 10 Dec. Coinbase is quoted: "Under MiCA, Coinbase is required to terminate the USDC rewards program for EEA customers." One source cites **MiCA Article 50** (no interest on e-money tokens). The last rate before the shutdown was **4.35%** per one source, **up to 4.7%** per another. This is historical context (Dec 2024). ## 4. 2026 incidents hitting yield seekers (Kelp DAO, MetaMask staking, Q3 2026 hack totals)

### Takeaway
**Kelp DAO (18 Apr 2026)** was a cross-chain *bridge verification* failure, not a classic smart-contract bug. About **$292M** was taken (roughly 18% of rsETH's circulating supply, as reported), and the attacker's use of stolen rsETH as collateral on Aave V3 created an estimated **$124M–$230M** bad-debt risk for Aave (scenario estimates, not settled). **MetaMask's staking incident (30 Sep–1 Oct 2026)** forced the exit of a reported ~17,000 validators; direct loss reported as tiny (~0.36 ETH of diverted block rewards), so it is an operational-risk event, not a loss event, for stakers. Q3 2026 hack totals: **CertiK counts $1.26B lost across 247 incidents (+53.9% vs Q2's $819.4M)**; PeckShield's Q3 total is reported at ~$1.14B (weaker outlet). The **Bitget breach ($387.5M, a centralised exchange)** and the **Liquid Network exploit (~$320M)** dominate, and **September 2026 was the worst month of the year (~$766–768M)**. No Immunefi or Chainalysis Q3 figure was found.

### Cited Findings
#### Kelp DAO exploit (18 Apr 2026)
- **Size and date:** about **$292M** lost on 18 Apr 2026, "2026's biggest crypto exploit", with wrapped ether "stranded across 20 chains". The first-pass notes said $293M; sources give $292M (CoinDesk, OpenZeppelin headlines) and $293M (usethebitcoin.com). → **corrected to ~$292M (±$1M, rounding)**. — [CoinDesk, 19 Apr 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/04/19/2026-s-biggest-crypto-exploit-kelp-dao-hit-for-usd292-million-with-wrapped-ether-stranded-across-20-chains); [OpenZeppelin, "$292 Million Lost, Zero Bugs Found"](https://www.openzeppelin.com/news/lessons-from-kelpdao-hack); [usethebitcoin.com ($293M)](https://usethebitcoin.com/news/kelp-dao-293m-exploit/)
- **Share of supply:** the attacker took roughly **18% of rsETH's circulating supply** (reported in the search summary; the specific page is uncertain among Halborn, Genfinity and KuCoin). — [Halborn, "Explained: The Kelp DAO Hack"](https://www.halborn.com/blog/post/explained-the-kelp-dao-hack-april-2026); [Genfinity, 20 Apr 2026](https://genfinity.io/2026/04/20/kelp-dao-rseth-292-million-exploit-layerzero-bridge-defi-contagion/)
- **Mechanism (sources differ):** OpenZeppelin's headline says "zero bugs found" and the summary says every contract operated as designed. Per LayerZero (as reported), attackers **compromised two RPC nodes** used by LayerZero Labs' decentralised verifier network (DVN) and **DDoS'd the remaining clean nodes** to force failover to the poisoned ones, so a fake cross-chain message was accepted. — [OpenZeppelin](https://www.openzeppelin.com/news/lessons-from-kelpdao-hack); [Halborn](https://www.halborn.com/blog/post/explained-the-kelp-dao-hack-april-2026)
- **Blame:** LayerZero says Kelp's **1-of-1 DVN** configuration enabled the exploit; Kelp's memo reportedly says it followed LayerZero's documentation and default configuration. **LayerZero preliminarily linked the attack to North Korea's Lazarus Group.** — [The Block, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-04-20-kelp-dao-shifts-blame-layerzero-398204); [Blockhead, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.blockhead.co/2026/04/20/kelp-dao-loses-292m-in-largest-defi-exploit-of-2026-layerzero-attributes-attack-to-lazarus-group/)

### Inferences
- Rising network revenue and falling token prices are both true at once, so per-participant income depends on the token price on the day of sale. The two Messari datapoints (HNT −77% and GEOD −41% in 2025, revenue up ~8x and ~1.7x) suggest that **USD value per token paid to operators fell during 2025** even as the networks sold more services. That is an inference; per-device USD earnings for 2026 were not found.
- Where per-device numbers exist they come from guides and blogs, not from the networks, and they span orders of magnitude: Helium home hotspot $0.05–5/month; Grass $5–20/month; Bee Maps single-digit to low double-digit dollars/month; io.net $110–220/month per GPU card at high utilisation (unverified); GEODNET $1.8/day per station (early-2025 estimate). Hardware cost matters: **CALCUL (illustrative)**: if the $589 upfront Bee price is the device price, recovering it at ~$10–20/month would take ~29–59 months, before any change in the HONEY price.
- Network-level revenue (Helium ~$15.6M annualised, io.net ~$12.5M annualised, Grass ~$17M, sector $72M in 2025) is tiny next to the multi-billion market caps (sector ~$10B per Messari), which is why token emissions, not customer revenue, still fund most participant income. This is my reading of the figures above, not a claim by a source.
