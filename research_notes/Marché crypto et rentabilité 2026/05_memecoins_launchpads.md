## 1. State of the memecoin market in 2026: size, volumes vs. the late-2024/Jan-2025 peak, dominant chains, "seasons"

### Takeaway
In October 2026 the memecoin sector is a fraction of its late-2024 peak. CoinGecko's meme category was about $34–35B in early October 2026, after a low near $24.5B in mid-June 2026, against roughly $135–150B at the Nov–Dec 2024 peak. That is still about −74% from the top despite a summer/autumn rebound. Solana still dominates on-chain memecoin trading. BNB Chain (its "meme season" in October 2025) and Base (Zora creator coins) are secondary venues, and Robinhood Chain emerged as a new venue in 2026. 2026 has had short bursts (January, March, August, early October) rather than a full "season".

## 2. Launchpads: pump.fun economics and the competitive landscape in 2026

### Takeaway
Pump.fun is still the dominant and most profitable launchpad. It earned about $322M in 2026 year-to-date, second among all crypto protocols behind Hyperliquid, after about $971M of gross revenue in 2025. Its 2026 run-rate is roughly 45–65% below 2025. About 25,000–40,000 tokens launch per day, and only about 1–3% graduate from the bonding curve (the graduation threshold is about $69K market cap). Pump.fun has recycled much of its revenue into PUMP buybacks (about $482M cumulative, plus a 36% supply burn in April 2026), yet the buyback model was publicly judged to have failed to support the price. LetsBonk briefly overtook pump.fun in mid-2025. I found no reliable 2026 market-share figures for any launchpad.

### Inferences
- **Revenue trajectory:** $971M (2025) against about $322M YTD 2026 over about 9.4 months gives roughly $410M annualised (my arithmetic). That is consistent with the dashboard's run-rate of about $452M (Sept 2026), i.e. 2026 revenue about 45–60% below 2025. Pump.fun is still one of the most profitable businesses in crypto: the platform keeps earning in a downturn.
- **Base rate for a new token:** launch volume is still about 25,000–40,000 per day while graduation is about 1–3%, so 97% or more of tokens never reach the AMM. "Graduating" is itself a low bar (about $69K market cap).
- Graduation rates depend heavily on method and product features: 0.26% in mid-June, 1.7% in July, then 2.7–3% in September after BOOST. Any single figure needs its date and method.
- **Platform revenue is not the same as token-holder returns.** More than $480M of buybacks and a 36% supply burn did not keep PUMP supported; CoinDesk's headline says the model "fails to support price".
- **Creator economics:** creator fees of about $20M in Q3 2026 are small next to platform revenue (about $100M+ per quarter at the run-rate). Spread over roughly 2.3–3.7M launches per quarter (25,000–40,000 per day), that averages about $5–9 per launch (my arithmetic, approximate). The real distribution is surely extremely skewed towards a few successful creators.
- **Trading terminals may now out-earn the launchpad:** in Q3 2026 Axiom (about $85.3M) and GMGN (about $104.7M) together earned about $190M (see §6), against about $113M per quarter for pump.fun at the September run-rate (my arithmetic). Caveats: GMGN is multichain and the revenue definitions differ.

## 4. Insider dynamics: bundling, sniping, dev dumps, cabals, KOLs, celebrity and political memecoins

### Takeaway
Most available evidence comes from small samples, forensic case studies and post-mortems, and it points one way: launchpad profits go disproportionately to insiders who buy in the first block (bundlers, snipers, devs) and to MEV bots, while KOL followers and late buyers absorb the losses. TRUMP is the largest-scale example. By the end of June 2026, about 989k of 1.48M buyer wallets had lost a combined $3.81B, a minority of early buyers held $4.04B of gains, and Donald Trump disclosed $636M of earnings from the coin. On LIBRA (Milei), the US class action was dismissed in October 2026 and the US DOJ declined to assist the Argentine courts.

### Cited Findings
**Bundling, sniping and coordinated dumps (2026)**
- **memescope (6–8 Oct 2026; 390 Solana and 109 Robinhood Chain launches):**
  - **23.6% of pump.fun launches had a bundled first block**, meaning 2 or more non-creator wallets bought in the creation slot.
  - **33.6% showed a coordinated dump**, meaning 3 or more early buyers sold within 5 seconds.
  - **Insiders captured 59–83% of realised profit per launch day.**

  Source: [memescope GitHub (Oct 2026)](https://github.com/AIpiggyyy/memescope) [self-published, small sample, unverified; I could not open the repo to check definitions]
- **Mechanism:** in a "bundle", the deployer buys a large share of supply across many wallets in the same block as the launch. The holder list then looks distributed while one entity controls the float. Snipers do the same moments later — [Meme Central guide](https://memecentral.fun/guides/bundled-launches-and-sniper-wallets). A rule of thumb: if same-block buyers hold more than about 15–20% of the float, treat the launch as bundled until proven otherwise — [Blockcircle](https://blockcircle.com/blog/detect-bundled-meme-coin-launches) [heuristic, not a standard]
- Bonding curves give the earliest buyers a significant first-mover advantage, and in high-risk launches the earliest buyers acquire a substantial share of supply — [MemeTrans, arXiv, Feb 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480v1)

**MEV and sandwich bots (2026)**
- **Bitquery study (reported October 2026):**
  - **6,850,144 sandwich attacks** between 30 Oct 2025 and 7 Oct 2026.
  - Carried out by **2,545 bot wallets** against **2,154,994 unique victim wallets**.
  - About **$29.7M extracted**.
  - About **96% hit tokens on launch curves and PumpSwap**.

  Source: [KuCoin News Insight (Oct 2026), relaying CryptoSlate/Bitquery](https://www.kucoin.com/news/insight/SOL/6acd589ff25df5000749ae17)
- A larger but less rigorous estimate: sandwich bots extracted $370–500M over about 16 months, with wide multi-slot sandwiches making up 93% of activity — [DEV Community blog (2026)](https://dev.to/ohmygod/solana-mev-defense-in-2026-how-sandwich-bots-extracted-500m-and-the-6-protocol-level-defenses-16d9) [weak]. HIST.: a single validator reportedly extracted more than $60M in one month in 2025 — [CryptoWeekly](https://cryptoweekly.co/news/mev-sandwich-attacks-lead-to-60m-being-extracted-via-solana-validator/)
- Memecoin traders are especially exposed because they set high slippage tolerances on illiquid, volatile tokens — [Helius Solana MEV report (date not confirmed)](https://www.helius.dev/blog/solana-mev-report)

**KOLs and influencers**
- Luo et al. (2026 paper as summarised by Emergent Mind) built a multi-agent system to identify genuinely profitable KOL wallets. It reached about 70% precision but low recall, on 1,000 launched memecoins and 614,330 wallet–coin pairs. In other words, telling skilled KOLs apart is hard — [Emergent Mind topic page](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/key-opinion-leader-kol-wallets) [original paper not verified]

### Inferences
- **The same pattern at every scale:** from three days of pump.fun launches (insiders taking 59–83% of realised profit) up to TRUMP (early buyers +$4.04B, later buyers −$3.81B, issuer +$636M), memecoin returns are mostly a **transfer from late and retail buyers to issuers, insiders and the fastest actors**. Operators control many wallets, so the per-operator take is higher. This is a small but systematic levy on traders who use high slippage.
- **Little legal accountability for insiders so far:** the LIBRA civil case was dismissed in the US (October 2026), the DOJ declined to help, and in the pump.fun case the Solana-ecosystem defendants were reportedly dismissed (§5).

## 5. Lawsuits and regulation

### Takeaway
In the US, SEC staff (February 2025) treat memecoins as collectible-like assets outside securities law, so private litigation is the main accountability channel. The pump.fun class action (Aguilar v. Baton Corp., SDNY) was still active in October 2026. On 31 Aug 2026 the claims against the Solana-ecosystem defendants were reportedly dismissed with prejudice, leaving RICO claims against pump.fun's operator and founders. The LIBRA class action against Hayden Davis was dismissed in October 2026. I found no 2026 SEC enforcement action targeting memecoins, and I could not research EU/France.

### Inferences
- **Ranked by strength of evidence:**
  1. Launchpads and terminals: on-chain fee data show persistent profits even in a downturn.
  2. MEV bots: consistent, but a small and concentrated total.
  3. Insiders, bundlers and devs: forensic samples show a consistent edge, but the methods often amount to deception or fraud (faking a distributed holder base, coordinated dumps, undisclosed promotion) and carry legal risk.
  4. Token creators: negligible on average, with a lottery-like distribution.
  5. KOL marketing: no hard data on earnings.
  6. Ordinary traders: negative expected value once fees, MEV and rugs are counted. Small wins make up most of the positive tail.
- **Rough size of the take (my arithmetic, mixed definitions):** pump.fun (about $322M YTD), Axiom (about $211M, Q1–Q3) and GMGN (about $171M, Q1–Q3) together took **about $700M from traders in 2026 so far**. GMGN is multichain and not memecoin-only. By contrast, **creators received about $57M** in Q1–Q3 2026, roughly 8% of that.
- **Survivorship bias is everywhere in the "profit" narrative:** Kolscan leaderboards, PnL screenshots and the "73% profitable" headline all filter for winners or realised trades, and omit wallets that quit or still hold worthless tokens.
- **For a French retail reader:** the evidence puts memecoin trading closer to a negative-sum lottery than an investment strategy. The people who make money reliably are platforms, bots and insiders.

### Gaps
- No hard data on liquidity-provider returns in memecoin pools (for example Meteora DLMM).
- No hard data on KOL earnings (paid promotion fees, allocation deals).
- No independent audit of how sniper-bot profits are distributed, i.e. how many sniper operators are net profitable after costs.
- Terminal fee schedules (Axiom, GMGN, priority fees, Jito tips) were not verified, so the full round-trip cost for retail is not quantified.

## 7. 2026 trends (new launchpad models, creator coins, AI agents, attention tokens) and community sentiment

### Takeaway
2026 is a post-mania phase. Launchpads are adding mechanisms aimed at keeping traders: anti-sniper and fair-launch designs, BOOST buy-and-burn at migration, creator-fee sharing, and AI-agent "Mayhem Mode". Creator coins went multichain (Zora on Base, Solana and Robinhood Chain; creator coins in the Base App), and Robinhood Chain emerged as a meme venue. Sentiment is broadly sceptical, with headlines such as "meme coin season won't return" and "pump.fun dead", and the trader base is smaller and more experienced. I could not gather 2026 evidence on ICM ("internet capital markets", e.g. Believe), "attention" tokens, broader AI-agent tokens or Reddit sentiment.
