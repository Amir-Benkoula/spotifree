## 1. Top protocols/apps by fees and revenue (30d / 90d / annualized / YTD 2026), and which categories dominate

### Takeaway
Stablecoin issuers capture by far the most revenue of any on-chain-tracked entity (DefiLlama 30-day revenue ≈ **$518M for Tether** and ≈ **$211M for Circle**), roughly 10× the top DeFi app, **Hyperliquid (≈ $70M of 30-day revenue; ≈ $429M YTD to mid-September 2026)**, followed by pump.fun, Polymarket and the Axiom terminal. The whole crypto revenue pie, including off-chain exchanges and brokers, was **$47B in H1 2026, −23% YoY** (1kx), and the share going to blockchains themselves fell to a historic low of 25%.

## 2. Stablecoin issuers: Tether, Circle and others

### Takeaway
Tether remains the most profitable company in crypto (**> $10B net profit in 2025**), but its 2026 run-rate is much lower (**Q1 ≈ $1.04B, Q2 ≈ $1.5B net operating profit**) and its excess-reserve buffer was **halved to $4.11B at 30 June 2026** because of gold/BTC mark-to-market. Circle's Q2 2026 revenue was **$701M (+7% YoY)** but net income only **$48M**, because most reserve income is paid out to distribution partners; USDC supply fell quarter-on-quarter to $73.3B.

### Cited Findings
**Tether — 2025 full year ([HIST] baseline)**

- Gold holdings > **$17B** and bitcoin ≈ **$8B** at end-2025 — [CoinDesk, 30 Jan 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/01/30/tether-s-gold-holdings-top-usd17-billion-as-net-profits-surpassed-usd10-billion-for-2025)
- Q4 2025 "records" headline: ≈ $187B USDT, 534M users, $4.4T moved (headline only) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tether-smashes-q4-records-crypto-071948293.html)

**Other issuers**
- Sky (ex-MakerDAO; USDS/DAI): ≈ **$209M** of revenue in a mid-2026 DeFi ranking (window not specified) [WEAK] — [Coinmonks/Medium](https://medium.com/coinmonks/highest-revenue-defi-protocols-in-2026-who-actually-makes-money-onchain-1c5ea77f8cf3)

### Inferences
- Circle's RLDC margin guidance (~42–44%) implies **~56–58% of its revenue goes to distribution and transaction costs** (largely to Coinbase): stablecoin economics are shared with distributors, while Tether keeps nearly all of its yield. Circle's net margin in Q2 2026 ≈ 6.8% ($48M / $701M).
- Tether's Q2 2026 operating profit (~$1.5B) is ≈ **30×** Circle's Q2 net income ($48M) with only ≈ 2.5× the float (≈ $183.6B liabilities vs $73.3B USDC).
- Tether H1 2026 net operating profit ≈ **$2.5B** (1.04 + 1.5) vs > $10B for FY2025 — but 2025 is "net profit" including investment gains while 2026 figures are "net operating profit"; not strictly comparable, though the direction (sharply lower) is clear.
- DefiLlama's Tether 30-day revenue (~$518M, ≈ $1.55B/quarter) is close to the attested Q2 operating profit (~$1.5B), suggesting Tether's operating costs are small relative to reserve income.
- Tether's equity buffer is now driven by volatile gold/BTC marks (−$4.1B in one quarter despite ~$1.5B of operating profit): its "profit" is increasingly a function of gold and bitcoin prices, not only T-bill yield.

## 3. Centralized exchanges and brokers (Coinbase, Robinhood, Kraken, Bullish, Gemini, Binance; spot vs derivatives share)
