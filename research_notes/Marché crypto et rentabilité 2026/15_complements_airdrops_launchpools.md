## 1. Which major airdrops happened in 2025-2026, and what were they worth at distribution vs now?

### Takeaway
(in progress) Early result: three of the most hyped "pending" airdrops (MetaMask, Base, Polymarket) had NOT happened as of the latest sources found. No credible ranking of realised 2026 airdrops by value was found; the rankings available are all-time lists dominated by Hyperliquid (Nov 2024).

### Cited Findings
- All-time ranking by value at the token's ATH: Hyperliquid first at $23.82B, Uniswap $6.75B, ApeCoin $4.01B (reported by Airdrops.io, "15 biggest airdrops ever"; date not established; historical context, not 2026). — [Airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/blog/biggest-airdrops-ever/)
- CoinGecko's own list ("50 Biggest Crypto Airdrops: $26.6B in 'Free Money'") puts Uniswap first at about $6.43B, then ApeCoin and dYdX; the two rankings differ because they value airdrops differently (reported by search synthesis; date not established). — [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/biggest-crypto-airdrops)
- No 2026 "ranking of realised airdrops" was found. 2026 articles (Datawallet, Bitget Academy, CryptoRank "Upcoming airdrops 2026", 99bitcoins, Zipmex, Webopedia May 2026, Bitcoin Foundation Sep 2026) are mostly forward-looking or affiliate-style guides with estimated per-user ranges. — [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/blog/upcoming-crypto-airdrops-in-2026); [Webopedia, May 2026](https://www.webopedia.com/news/markets/best-crypto-airdrops-in-may-2026/); [Bitcoin Foundation, Sep 2026](https://bitcoinfoundation.org/news/analysis/best-crypto-airdrops-2026/)

**Did NOT happen (as of the latest sources found; to be re-checked against any later result below)**
- MetaMask (MASK): no confirmed TGE, date or claim rules. Consensys CEO Joseph Lubin said in September 2025 that the token is coming. One CoinGape article claims "MASK airdrop now live" via maskfoundation.info, but it is the only source saying so and the domain is not MetaMask's; treat as a likely scam/phishing claim. A MEXC guide calls Q3-Q4 2026 the "most realistic" window (speculation). — [CoinGape (claim)](https://coingape.com/metamask-token-launch-mask-airdrop-now-live-for-eligible-wallets/); [BingX](https://bingx.com/en/learn/article/what-is-metamask-airdrop-how-to-participate-earn-mask-rewards); [CoinCodex](https://coincodex.com/article/73631/metamask-mask-token-airdrop/); [MEXC blog](https://blog.mexc.com/token-reviews/metamask-mask-airdrop-guide-2026-how-to-position-for-the-biggest-potential-token-event-in-crypto-history/)
- Base network token: not announced; Base said at BaseCamp 2025 that it is "exploring" a token. Guides citing a Q2-Q4 2026 window are unsourced speculation. — [KuCoin Base airdrop guide 2026](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/base-airdrop-guide-2026-how-to-claim-base-tokens); [MEXC blog](https://blog.mexc.com/token-reviews/base-network-token-farming-guide-2026-how-to-build-on-chain-history-before-coinbases-l2-potentially-distributes/)
- Polymarket (POLY): no TGE or airdrop announced; the Polymarket help center says it has not announced any airdrop or TGE. CMO Matthew Modabber said on a podcast in October 2025 that a token and airdrop are coming, tied to the US relaunch. Several earlier speculative dates (including mid-2026) passed without a launch. — [Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/learning-hub/post/polymarket-token-launch/); [BingX](https://bingx.com/en/learn/article/what-is-polymarket-poly-token-when-will-it-launch); [99bitcoins](https://99bitcoins.com/analysis/polymarket-airdrop/)

- A 2026 "airdrop ranking" can only be built from individual project data; none of the guides is reliable for it.
