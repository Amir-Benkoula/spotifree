*Research conditions (read first). (1) WebSearch only, 22 of 22 searches used, all in "extended" mode; WebFetch and curl were unavailable, so no page was opened directly. Every figure comes from the search engine's synthesis of the cited page. Each figure is therefore attributed to the page the synthesis named, and "reported by" marks figures seen only in a synthesis. Publication dates appear only when visible in the URL or the synthesis; otherwise "date not stated". (2) "Computed" marks arithmetic I did on sourced inputs. (3) This file is an addendum to `06_ventes_tokens_ico_airdrops.md` (not edited). Where I re-checked a first-pass lead, the verdict (CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / COULD NOT VERIFY) is stated; the full list is at the end of section 5. (4) Absence of evidence in search results is not proof that an event did not happen; this is worded accordingly. (5) Several data points come from Bitquery, a blockchain-data vendor whose studies are self-published content marketing; the numbers are on-chain-derived but I could not audit the method.*

## 1. Which major airdrops happened in 2025-2026, and what were they worth at distribution vs now?

### Takeaway
Among the airdrops whose value could be tracked, only Hyperliquid (Nov 2024, a benchmark outside the 2025-26 window) multiplied in value. Monad's airdrop is worth about 18-24% less than on launch day, and Linea's about 87-94% less (computed). Lighter's is about 34-47% above its day-one value but only after a -71% drawdown (first-pass data). Systematically, Bitquery's study of 51 large 2026 airdrops finds 35 of 46 evaluable drops below their first-week price (median -48%). MetaMask, Base, Polymarket and OpenSea had no launch in any source found as of 13 Oct 2026. No credible ranking of realised 2026 airdrops exists; the available rankings are all-time lists dominated by Hyperliquid.

**Systematic 2026 evidence (Bitquery, on-chain, data through late September 2026)**
- Bitquery tracked 51 large 2026 airdrops on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Arbitrum, each paying at least 5,000 wallets and at least $1M, covering 2.02 million wallets and $809M at claim-day prices. ### Inferences
- The decisive variable is the sell date. Bitquery shows most claimers sell within a day, and the median drop later trades 48% below its first-week price, so quick sellers captured more than holders in most drops. Hyperliquid, supported by buybacks, is the exception.
- Monad is an airdrop of a token whose public sale had already priced it near the market: it debuted near its sale price (Decrypt) and now sits roughly at it, so there was little upside for airdrop recipients beyond the day-one level.
- The headline figures ($105M, $809M, $675M) are aggregates. Bitquery's mean is about $400 per wallet ($809M / 2.02M, computed), and since the top 10% of claimers took 73% of tokens in the median drop, the typical claimer received much less.
- Survivorship bias: ranking articles sort by value at ATH, which is dominated by a handful of outliers (Hyperliquid, Uniswap). The typical 2025-26 airdrop looks like Linea or the median Bitquery drop.

### Gaps
- Current (Oct 2026) prices for KAITO, BERA and ASTER were not retrieved, and the HYPE $91.65 figure has uncertain attribution.
- A Hyperliquid Season 2 distribution is unverified. SEA's launch status after the delay is unverified.
- Per-wallet median airdrop values (as opposed to means) were not found.

## 2. Exchange programs: Binance Alpha, HODLer Airdrops/Launchpool, Bybit/OKX/Bitget; French availability

### Takeaway
Binance Alpha points farming is a volume-generation game with a very low measured friction cost (about 2 cents per $100 traded, Bitquery, Aug-Sep 2026), and the rules keep tightening; no reliable 2026 "daily earnings" figure was found. Airdrops distributed through Binance Wallet were mostly dumped within a day. Since 1 Jul 2026 Binance no longer serves French clients, which closes its account-based programs (Launchpool, HODLer Airdrops) to French users. Whether the non-custodial Binance Wallet/Alpha flow is still usable from France was not verified. Bybit/OKX/Bitget programs exist, but no verified 2026 yields were found.

### Cited Findings
**Binance Alpha points**
- Bitquery's on-chain study (Aug-Sep 2026) found 1,707 BNB Chain wallets trading about $4.76 billion of a token worth under $2 million, stopping near Alpha points thresholds. Estimated cost: about 2 cents per $100 of volume (median gap between what a wallet paid and what it got back); one analysis, one dataset, not a general rate. Computed: about $0.95M in total friction cost, roughly $560 per wallet. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/binance-alpha-points-farming)
- Rules, per Bitquery citing Binance's points FAQ updated 9 Jul 2026: volume points come from purchases only; 2 USD of volume = 1 point, plus 1 point per doubling; totals are computed daily at 23:59:59 UTC over the last 15 days; selling does not reduce points; since 22 Oct 2025, buys count only once wallet balance points reach the equivalent of $100. Binance Wallet's terms (version dated 23 Sep 2026) ban manipulation aimed at accumulating points one is not entitled to. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/binance-alpha-points-farming)
- Earlier rule changes: from 6 Jun 2025 only purchases made with Alpha tokens or Binance Spot-listed tokens earned volume points; a 17 Jun 2025 change stopped points on Alpha-to-Alpha swaps; bot use is treated as a violation (loss of eligibility, API revocation, trading limits). — [Binance Wallet on X](https://x.com/BinanceWallet/status/1930626361263358313); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/binance-alpha-bot-crackdown-token-points); [Techpression](https://techpression.com/binance-penalises-accounts-using-bot-to-farm-alpha-points/)
- CryptoRank published a story on a "mass sell-off" causing a Binance Alpha "meltdown" and on whether Binance's reform could restore trust (publication date not established). — [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/496ca-can-binance-alphas-reform-restore-trust)
- Dated marketing claim, not a 2026 figure: a PANews guide of roughly mid-2025 headlined "Earn 30,000 RMB a month" for Alpha points; the same period's balance-point tiers (1 to 4 points per day depending on holdings) predate later rule changes. I treat these as unverified marketing. — [PANews](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/8q6aqg657g8u); [Smartwallet guide](https://smartwallet.com.ua/en/blog/complete-guide-to-binance-alpha-points)

**Binance HODLer Airdrops / Launchpool**
- Allora (ALLO) was announced as the "58th HODLer Airdrop" (date not stated), with 15M tokens backing BNB Earn subscriptions, so the program has run for dozens of listings. — [CryptoNinjas](https://www.cryptoninjas.net/news/binance-lists-allora-allo-as-58th-hodler-airdrop-with-15m-tokens-backing-bnb-earn-subscriptions/)

**Bybit, OKX, Bitget (descriptions only; no verified 2026 yields)**

### Inferences
- With about 2 cents of friction per $100 of Alpha volume, the fee cost of points farming is small. Whether it pays depends on the value per point of the points-gated airdrops, plus slippage, capital tied up and ban risk. No per-point payout data was found, so profitability cannot be computed from the evidence.
- Because HODLer/Launchpool depend on a Binance account, French residents appear unable to join new ones since 1 Jul 2026 (inference from the Euronews/Cryptonomist reports; the exact scope for Earn products was not stated in the snippets).
- Day-one selling was heavy across all channels: the median of the 51 drops had 73% of claimers moving tokens within a day, and the Binance Wallet router figures (43% of 754,153 claimers within a day; 50-70% in four drops) are in the same range. No channel-specific conclusion (Binance vs other venues) is warranted from these data.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 daily-earnings range for Alpha farming, no per-point value, and no capital requirement beyond the $100 balance threshold.
- No 2026 yields for Binance Launchpool, Bybit Launchpool, OKX Jumpstart or Bitget Launchpool.
- A search synthesis mentioned that NIGHT (Midnight Network), a Binance post-listing drop, fell shortly after trading began, attributed to rapid distribution of free tokens and early profit-taking; the page it came from was not identified, so I do not rely on it.
- MiCA/French status of Bybit, OKX, Bitget, and whether Binance Wallet remains usable from France: not checked.

## 7. Airdrop farming ROI vs costs, sybil-filtering evidence, survivorship bias

### Takeaway
No study found measures net airdrop-farmer ROI. The indirect evidence is unflattering for the typical farmer: a mean claim of about $400 per wallet at claim-day prices (computed), high concentration in the top 10% of claimers, 73% of claimers selling within a day, and retroactive sybil filtering that makes eligibility unpredictable. Costs such as the Alpha friction (about $560 per wallet in one dataset, computed) are real while payouts are uncertain. Rankings of "biggest airdrops" suffer from survivorship bias.

### Cited Findings
- **Payout size and concentration (Bitquery, 51 large 2026 airdrops):** $809M across 2.02M wallets at claim-day prices (mean about $400 per wallet, computed); the top 10% of claimers took 73% of tokens in the median drop; 73% of claimers moved tokens within a day; only 23% still held at least half after a month; median drop -48% vs first-week price. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/crypto-airdrops-2026)
- **Cost side (Alpha):** about 2 cents per $100 of volume, so about $0.95M for $4.76B across 1,707 wallets (computed, about $560 per wallet), plus capital, ban risk and the 15-day rolling window. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/binance-alpha-points-farming)
- **Retroactive sybil filtering:** a Block3 Finance essay describes the common model: anyone may participate, and sybil wallets are excluded retroactively, so users cannot know whether their activity will count until the end. — [Block3 Finance](https://www.block3finance.com/understanding-sybil-attacks-in-airdrops)
- **Scale of exclusion (weak sources):** guides claim 85% of new airdrops now filter out sybil farms; one 2026 article says a major Layer-2 drop disqualified about 40% of addresses (project unnamed in the snippet); Optimism reportedly excluded about 17,000 addresses from its first distribution (historical; the year was not stated in the snippet). These come from blogs and vendor content and I found no primary disclosure. — [Block3 Finance](https://www.block3finance.com/understanding-sybil-attacks-in-airdrops); [WhySoGeek](https://whysogeek.com/crypto-airdrop-farming-sybil-safety-2026/)
- **Project disclosures found:** Meteora's MET airdrop used sybil detection, related-wallet grouping and blocklists (claim window ends 21 Oct 2026). MegaETH's sale drew public sybil concerns. — [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/meteora-airdrop-met-claim-deadline-october); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/megaeth-mega-token-sale-surpasses-111927755.html)
- **Academic:** a 2026 paper in the International Journal of Electronics and Telecommunications proposes a machine-learning framework to detect airdrop sybil attacks; the abstract notes that tokens from one airdrop can reach thousands of dollars for an effective hunter. I could not see the paper's results. Related work: "Airdrops: Giving Money Away Is Harder Than It Seems" (arXiv 2312.02752) and the TierDrop paper (Yaish and Livshits, arXiv 2407.01176, 2024); I only saw them as references. — [IJET](https://ijet.pl/index.php/ijet/article/view/10.24425-ijet.2026.157879/3120); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.02752)
- **Detection methods named:** clustering by shared funding, identical activity and timing; IP and browser-fingerprint signals (the latter described in a piece by an anti-detect-browser company, so commercially biased). One 2026 blog post is titled "Airdrop Farming in 2026: Sybil Filters Killed the Playbook". — [WhySoGeek](https://whysogeek.com/crypto-airdrop-farming-sybil-safety-2026/); [Multilogin guide on ScribeHow](https://scribehow.com/page/Multilogin_for_Airdrop_Farming_2026_Beat_Sybil_Detection_Across_Wallets__L7HoefG5RMOtKETEWDa2Hg); [EnigmaProxy (proxy vendor)](https://enigmaproxy.net/blog/proxy-strategies-crypto-airdrop-farming-sybil-detection)

### Inferences
- Net farmer ROI cannot be computed from the sources. A mean of about $400 per wallet at claim-day prices implies a positive but modest payout for a genuine user with near-zero cost who sells quickly (the median drop later trades 48% below its first-week price). Because the top 10% of claimers took 73% of tokens, the median payout is well below the mean. Costs of capital, gas, fees, time and multi-wallet management, plus sybil exclusion risk, could plausibly exceed that for deliberate farmers (inference; no cost dataset found), and large farmers who beat the filters would capture a disproportionate share (concentration data).
- Survivorship bias: (1) all-time rankings sort by value at ATH, which is dominated by Hyperliquid and Uniswap; (2) social-media anecdotes ("$13,000 for a $0.1 deposit", one whale at $78M) show winners only; (3) Bitquery's sample excludes small airdrops (below 5,000 wallets or $1M) and non-EVM chains; (4) vendor and affiliate guides profit from farming signups.
- For French residents in particular, the Binance route is closed (section 2), and most 2026 airdrops are on EVM chains reachable without an exchange, but no jurisdiction-specific tax or eligibility data was collected.

### Gaps
- No dataset of actual farmer cost and revenue, no percentage of sybil-excluded wallets from a primary project disclosure, and no evidence on what excluded farmers do next. Median (rather than mean) payout per wallet is not available. Tax treatment of airdrops in France was not researched in this pass.
